# 13. A cash settlement is recorded in the group's book, and the balances count it at once

## Context

Request 12 lets the payee of a payment in cash record it: `POST /groups/{id}/settlements/cash { from, amount }`, answered
`201 { id }` at once, and `GET /settlements/{id}`, answered `200` to a member of the group only. A settlement moves the
balances at once: the payer's goes up by its amount, the payee's down by it. It is never more than what the payer owes
nor more than what the payee is owed, at the moment it is recorded; two recorded at once are decided one after the other.

The storm (`conventions/storms/settle-in-cash.yaml`, decided with the product's owner) named the aggregate, the links and
the reads, and ADR 11 declared the Settlements context, its `SettlementBook` per group (T7), and the link that counts
settlements in Ledger's balances (T8, `SettledAmounts`), `planned` until the code carried it.

## Decision

- **A `SettlementBook` per group, keyed by the group's id.** The book is the aggregate every settlement of the group is
  decided in. Its identity is the id the group was born with, taken up as Groups announced it (DOMAIN-IDENTITY); the
  settlement's own id is generated by the book. Its state is its version, read with the group's book row; a book that has
  no row yet is at version 0.
- **A settlement is decided against the balances as they are, and saved against the book's version.** The use case reads
  the book first, then the balances, decides, and saves: the book's row is updated only where its version is still the one
  read (`CONC-OPTIMISTIC`), and a writer that lost the race gets a `ConcurrencyConflictException`, so the command re-reads
  in a new transaction (at most three attempts) and meets the balances the other settlement left. This is the one place a
  version is checked for settlements; expenses never contend for it.
- **The rule is the book's and refuses by name:** the amount is a whole number of at least 1 (`422`), the payer is a member
  of the group and not the payee (`404` for a payer who is no member, `422` for the payee paying itself), and the amount is
  at most `min(what the payer owes, what the payee is owed)` (`422`, `SettlementExceedsDebt`). An amount that is not a whole
  number is the body's shape (`400`).
- **Ledger counts settlements in every balance it reads** (T8): Ledger owns the port `SettledAmounts` and answers it through
  an adapter over Settlements' read model (`settledOf`, what each member paid and received in the group). `balancesOf` adds
  those amounts to the expenses' balances. The statement (request 8) still lists expenses only. Leaving (request 9) and the
  suggestions (request 11) read the same balances, so they count settlements too.
- **The payee records it;** the payer cannot claim a payment the payee never had (the storm's decision).
- **`SettlementRecorded` is the book's one domain event**, recorded by `recordCash()` and published after the commit, as the
  storm names it. No integration event carries it yet: no other context reacts to it in this request. The Lite profile
  records domain events only where an ADR says so, and this is that ADR.
- **Reading a settlement** is a query over Settlements' read model, answered to a member of its group only; for anyone
  else the settlement does not exist (`404`).

## Consequences

- The balances are read in two reads, the expenses and then the settlements, on two connections, as Ledger's read models
  already are; each is committed and each balances on its own, so the balances still add up to 0. A settlement and an
  expense recorded at the same moment are not ordered against each other; the book's version orders settlements only,
  which is the rule the storm decided (`Only settlements contend for it, never expenses`).
- A book row is created with the group's first settlement, `INSERT ... ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`, so two first settlements
  of a group at once meet the same check as any other two.
- `GetSettlement` and the suggestions read Settlements' read model and the members through the Groups' port, so a settlement
  of a group is seen by its members only.

## When to revisit

At request 13 (provider settlements, a payment pending until its webhook lands), where the book's status moves and the
balances must count a pending payment; and at request 14 (a refund), where the balance of a settlement can be undone.
